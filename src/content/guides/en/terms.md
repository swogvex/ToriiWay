## Learning tool, not an official test

The JLPT practice tests are an unofficial simulation. Their scores estimate your level, but they are not a JLPT result or a prediction of one. The site is not affiliated with, endorsed by or a substitute for the Japanese-Language Proficiency Test administered by the Japan Foundation and JEES.

## Fair use

- Use the site for learning, at a normal pace. Do not overload it, scrape it in bulk, or try to get around its limits or security checks.
- Leaderboard names must not be offensive, impersonate someone or contain personal details. Do not submit runs you did not play. Entries that break these rules can be hidden or removed, and their names blocked.
- Use the problem reports to point out mistakes, not to send unrelated messages.
