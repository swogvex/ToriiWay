## Who is responsible

The site is run by its owner, a private individual known as **swogvex**. For anything about your data, write to [git.swogvex@gmail.com](mailto:git.swogvex@gmail.com).

## No cookies, no tracking

The site sets no cookies and shows no ads. It uses no fingerprinting and no Google Analytics. Your choices and progress are kept in your browser's local storage, set only when you act. This includes the theme, the language, the side menu, your game settings and best scores, and JLPT runs in progress. We never see this data. You can clear it at any time from your browser's settings.

To count visits and measure speed we use Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights. Both work without cookies and only report totals, never a profile of you.

## What reaches our servers

- **JLPT practice runs.** Your answers, scores and section ratings, so the result page and the review work. If you choose to appear in the "recent results" list, the nickname you type is shown there. Runs are deleted 180 days after they start.
- **Results email (optional).** If you ask for your result by email, we store the address and use it once to send the link. It is erased 30 days after sending. We never use it for anything else.
- **Problem reports.** When you report a mistake in a question, we store the question, the category and your message. Please do not include personal details. Reports are kept until reviewed, then deleted 90 days after they were sent.
- **Kana Rush leaderboard.** If you choose to submit a run, we publish the name you pick (please don't use your real name), the script, difficulty, mode and the run's statistics. We don't store your IP address or your email. Your browser keeps a random key that lets it replace or remove your entry, and we only store a hash of that key. Entries stay until you remove them with "Remove my run" or ask us by email. Reports on entries are handled like problem reports.
- **Library clicks.** We count how many times each book is opened, per day. The count says nothing about who opened it.
- **Abuse protection.** To limit abuse, each request gets a key made from a salted hash of your IP address. The address itself is never stored, and the keys are deleted by a daily job after one day.

## Bot checks

Forms that could be abused (results email, problem reports, leaderboard submissions and reports) use Cloudflare Turnstile to tell people from bots. It loads only when you open one of those forms. To do its check, Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address and browser details.

## Videos

Videos on the Community page load from YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain (youtube-nocookie.com), and only after you press play. Until then your browser does not contact YouTube.

## Who helps run the site

- **Vercel** hosts the site and runs its server functions in Frankfurt (EU). It also provides the cookieless analytics and stores the audio files. Like any web host, it processes your IP address to deliver pages and keeps short technical logs.
- **Neon** hosts the database in the EU.
- **Resend** sends the results emails.
- **Cloudflare** provides Turnstile.

Vercel, Resend and Cloudflare are based in the United States. Transfers of data to them rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

## Why we may use this data

- **Your consent:** the results email and leaderboard entries. You give it by pressing "Send" or "Submit", and you can withdraw it at any time.
- **Legitimate interest:** running the practice tests, fixing reported mistakes, preventing abuse and counting visits in aggregate.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or take a copy of your data. You can also object to or restrict its use, and withdraw your consent. Write to [git.swogvex@gmail.com](mailto:git.swogvex@gmail.com). Because there are no accounts, tell us what helps find your data: the result link, the leaderboard name or the email address you used. We will answer within one month.

If you think your data has been mishandled, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency ([aepd.es](https://www.aepd.es)) or the authority in your country.

## Children

The site is open to learners of all ages, but it collects nothing that requires an account. If you are under 14, please ask a parent or guardian before sending an email address or a leaderboard name.

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
