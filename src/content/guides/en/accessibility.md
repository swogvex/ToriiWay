## Target

The site aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, in both light and dark themes and in English and Spanish.

## How it is checked

- Every change is tested automatically with axe on every type of page, in both themes, and with Lighthouse.
- Pages are checked with the keyboard alone: every control can be reached, shows a visible focus and works without a mouse. Headings, landmarks and labels are reviewed as screen readers present them.
- Japanese text is marked with its language, so screen readers can switch voice. If yours reads Japanese badly, the [Japanese voice guide](/help/japanese-voice) explains how to add one.
- Animations are reduced when your system asks for less motion.

## Known limits

- **Kana Rush** is a timed typing game built around moving characters, so it cannot be played with a screen reader. The same characters can be learned with the kana charts in [Learn](/learn/hiragana), which work fully with assistive technology.
- **JLPT listening questions** need sound. The review after a test shows the transcript of the listening questions you got wrong.
- **Stroke-order animations** are visual. Each kanji page also lists its stroke count and readings as text.

## Report a barrier

If something on the site is hard or impossible to use, write to [git.swogvex@gmail.com](mailto:git.swogvex@gmail.com) with the page address and what happened. We aim to reply within two weeks.
